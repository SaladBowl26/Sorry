To My Beautiful Rose 🌹❤️

Rose... Out of the billions of people in this world, somehow my heart chose you. And honestly? I'd choose you again in every lifetime.

You're my favorite notification. My favorite smile. My favorite laugh. My favorite person. My favorite everything.

You make my bad days feel lighter, my good days feel unforgettable, and every moment with you feels like home.

I don't know what I did to deserve someone like you, but I promise I'll spend forever being grateful for it.

You have the prettiest smile, the cutest laugh, the most beautiful eyes, and somehow... you still don't realize how amazing you are.

I don't want anyone else. I don't look for anyone else. Because the person I want is already reading this. ❤️

Every "I love you" I tell you comes from the deepest part of my heart. And somehow... I still feel like those three words aren't enough.

If I could have one wish, it would simply be... more time with you. More laughs. More hugs. More memories. More us.

You didn't just become my girlfriend... you became my safe place. My happiness. My peace.

No matter how many years pass, I hope we still laugh at dumb jokes, still annoy each other, still call each other cute names, and still love each other just as much.

So... ❤️

Will You Stay With Me Forever? 🌹

No pressure. No matter what... I'll always be thankful that I got to love someone as incredible as you. But if forever has a name... I hope it's us. ❤️
